# Phase 1 Presentation Deck Content — HOSPITALITY

## Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Title**: HOSPITALITY (Holistic Optimization System for Policy-Integrated Admission & Treatment Intelligence)
- **Tagline**: Empowering Indian Patients & Caregivers with Explainable Insurance-Aware Healthcare Navigation.
- **Challenge Track**: GE Healthcare Precision Care Challenge 2026.

## Slide 2: The Problem — The Financial Toxicity of Hospital Admission
- 70%+ of Indian health insurance disputes stem from proportionate deductions, room rent capping, and unexpected non-payable consumables.
- Fragmented data across public schemes (PM-JAY, CGHS), private insurers, and hospital rate cards leaves caregivers panicked during medical emergencies.

## Slide 3: The Innovation — Deterministic Insurance Math Meets Modern Health Standards
- **AI Extraction**: Converts unstructured policy legalese into typed FHIR R4 `Coverage` constraints with clause citations.
- **Deduction Simulator**: Eliminates hidden penalties by calculating exact room rent overage impacts on surgeon/OT fees.
- **Multi-Payer Network**: Unifies public schemes and private insurance empanelment networks into a single geospatial map.

## Slide 4: Architecture & Enterprise Data Sourcing
- Built on NRCeS FHIR R4, ABDM HFR standards, and NHCX interoperability guidelines.
- Authoritative baseline data from `data.gov.in`, `PM-JAY HBP 2022` (1,949 packages), and `CGHS` rate cards, augmented with transparently tagged synthetic bed feeds.

## Slide 5: Business Impact & Roadmap
- Reduces out-of-pocket billing shocks by up to 60%.
- Streamlines hospital admission workflows and accelerates TPA pre-auth turnarounds.
- Ready for ABDM M1-M3 certification and nationwide provider integration.
